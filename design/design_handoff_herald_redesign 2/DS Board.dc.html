01

Identity at scale

Colour names the account. Letters name the domain. Words name the mailbox.

An install can reach 8 accounts × 20 domains × 100 mailboxes: 16,000 mailboxes. Colour can tell about eight things apart, a two-letter mark a few hundred, and a name any number. Each level uses the cue that still works at that count.
